Help: shipping, returns and payment

Shipping

Orders over $40 ship free. Below that, delivery costs $3.

Delivery takes 1–2 business days inside Dhaka and 3–5 business days elsewhere in Bangladesh. We don't ship outside Bangladesh yet.

Returns

You can return unopened items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. If an opened product causes a skin reaction, contact us within 14 days and we'll refund it.

Start a return from the chat or email care@mirela.example with your order number.

Payment

We accept bKash, Nagad, Visa, Mastercard and cash on delivery.

Customer care

Our care team answers Saturday to Thursday, 10 am to 7 pm (Dhaka time), at care@mirela.example.
