Returns and refunds

Plants

If a plant arrives damaged, or dies within 14 days of delivery, send us a photo and we will replace it or refund it.

Pots

Unused pots can be returned within 30 days for a full refund. Return shipping for pots is paid by the customer, unless the pot arrived broken.

Gift wrap

Gift wrap fees are not refundable.
